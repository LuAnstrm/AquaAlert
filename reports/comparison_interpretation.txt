SECTION 2 – WHY ACCURACY IS NOT THE RIGHT PRIMARY METRIC
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Accuracy measures the fraction of all predictions that are correct. In a
balanced dataset this is a useful summary, but the AquaAlert dataset is
imbalanced: High-risk events make up only approximately 2 % of
observations. A model that labels every observation as 'Low' would
achieve very high accuracy while detecting zero floods. For a flood
warning system, the cost of a missed flood (a false negative) is
incomparably higher than the cost of a false alarm (a false positive),
so accuracy alone is a misleading guide.

SECTION 3 – THE DANGER OF FALSE NEGATIVES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
A FALSE NEGATIVE occurs when the model predicts Low or Moderate risk but
the true risk level is High. In the context of AquaAlert, a false
negative means: barangay residents are not warned, evacuation is not
triggered, and people remain in flood-prone areas during a dangerous
event. The direct consequence could be loss of life, injuries, or
preventable damage to property.

In the current test set, Logistic Regression missed 3 of 50 truly High-
risk events (High-recall = 0.94). Random Forest missed 1 of 50 (High-
recall = 0.98). Each missed event represents a potential failure to warn
a community.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Logistic Regression is the more interpretable model: each feature has a

SECTION 8 – LIMITATIONS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. THRESHOLD-DERIVED LABEL: The data is real (daily NCR observations
2016-2020), but its FloodOccurrence label equals rainfall >= 30 mm on
every row, and Moderate is defined by rainfall/river-level thresholds.
High scores show the models recover those thresholds, not validated
forecasting skill.

1b. NO TYPHOON-SCALE EVENTS: The wettest training day is about 53 mm.
Documented major Metro Manila floods (JICA/DPWH 2014, Table 2.6: Seniang
2000, Chedeng 2003, Winnie 2004, Ondoy 2009, Kirogi 2012) had 149-291 mm
of basin rainfall in one day. The models have never seen such an event.

2. SMALL HIGH-RISK SAMPLE: Only 50 High-risk rows in the test set. A
single correct or incorrect prediction changes recall by 0.02.
Confidence intervals are wide; treat all High-class metrics as
approximate.

3. NO TEMPORAL AUTOCORRELATION HANDLING: Flood risk in a city at time T
is correlated with risk at T−1. Neither model accounts for this
explicitly. A time-series model (LSTM, LightGBM with lag features) may
be more appropriate once enough real data is available.

4. PROTOTYPE ONLY: This system does not issue official flood warnings.
It is a student capstone decision-support prototype. Do not replace
PAGASA, LGU DRRMO, or NDRRMC advisories with its output.
